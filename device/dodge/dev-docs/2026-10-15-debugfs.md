# 2026-10-15 — debugfs summaries, screen awake

**Goal.** Read the clock, regulator, pinmux and gpio files that the
rooted capture missed.
**Phone.** CPH2653, build `CPH2653_16.0.10.501(EX01)`, slot `_a`. The
human had woken the screen.
**Kernel.** No kernel of ours. Stock OxygenOS, Magisk `su`.
**Result.** The four files are saved. debugfs was unmounted afterwards.

## What we did

`/proc/filesystems` lists `debugfs`. It was not mounted.
`/sys/kernel/debug` was an empty directory. tracefs was already mounted
at `/sys/kernel/tracing` and was left alone. As root:
`mount -t debugfs debugfs /sys/kernel/debug`, then `cat` of
`clk/clk_summary`, `regulator/regulator_summary`, `gpio`, and every
`pinctrl/*/pinmux-pins`. Then `umount /sys/kernel/debug`.

## What happened

Saved under
`~/android/reference/dodge-cph2653/rooted-2026-10-15-debugfs-awake/`,
out of git. Sizes: clk 178824, regulator 28268, gpio 15780, pinmux
20968. Each file starts with its usual header, not an error. After
unmount, `/sys/kernel/debug` is empty again. Nothing else under debugfs
was read.

## Learning

The missing files were missing because debugfs was not mounted, not
because the kernel lacks it. This set is the screen-awake view. A
screen-off copy of the regulator summary is still a separate pass, with
the panel turned off by hand before the mount.
