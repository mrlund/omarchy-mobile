## Power

Stock `cnss-peach` uses S5F and S4D from 876 mV, S7I from 1312 mV,
gpio 18 as `wcn_sw_ctrl` and gpio 19 as `wcn_sw`. Our tree muxed
gpio 18 as a plain GPIO with a pull-down, and the bucks sat on
their floors (852 mV and 1224 mV). L2F, L3F and S3G already
matched. The RF clock was already on.

Both tries reached the same log as the previous boot: TME-Lite
`err 83` about 200 ms before `ignore reset dev flags`, then
`qmi failed set mode request, mode: 7, err = -110`. The second
try set the switch pins to the stock functions before WLAN enable
went high, and held S7I at 1312 mV. S7I also feeds the UFS and
USB 1.2 V LDOs. Raising it did not disturb the desktop. ath12k
is bound. Do not load it again on this boot.

## Slot A

Nothing in this kernel marks the A/B slot successful. ABL allows
seven boots, then sets slot A unbootable and stays in fastboot.
`fastboot set_active a` (the slot that was already current) cleared
that and restored seven tries. Slot B was not changed. The success
bit lives in `boot_a`'s GPT attribute on `/dev/sde` (flag `0x37`,
success clear, six tries left after this boot). A write from Linux
returned `EPERM`. The storage guard stays as it is. Do not spend
the remaining tries on restarts until that bit can be set.

The Wi-Fi PCIe oneshot is enabled again. It still does not load
ath12k.
