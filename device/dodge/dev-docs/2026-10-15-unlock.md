# 2026-10-15 — flashing unlock accepted

**Goal.** `fastboot flashing unlock` after OEM unlocking was turned on.
**Phone.** CPH2653, build `CPH2653_16.0.10.501(EX01)`. OxygenOS, then
the bootloader, then the OxygenOS welcome slide.
**Kernel.** No kernel of ours.
**Result.** The command returned `OKAY`. The phone rebooted to the
setup welcome screen. `unlocked` has not been read back yet.

## What we did

Developer options had OEM unlocking on. adb still reported product
`CPH2653EEA`, model `CPH2653`, device `OP5D55L1`. USB id was `22d9:2769`.
`adb reboot bootloader`. Fastboot still showed `product:sun`,
`unlocked:no`, `secure:yes`, `current-slot:a`, slot B unbootable,
`is-userspace:no`. Then `fastboot flashing unlock`.

## What happened

The command finished in about 0.02 s with `OKAY`. The phone left USB.
It came back as `22d9:2764`, then `22d9:2769` with an adb interface.
`adb devices` listed it as a device, and `adb shell` returned
`error: closed` on every try. The screen was the OxygenOS start page
with a slide, which is the welcome step after a wipe.

## Learning

The first refusal was the OEM unlocking switch. With that switch on,
ABL accepts `fastboot flashing unlock` without a second fastboot
prompt. The welcome slide is the sign that userdata was erased.

The next screen insists on a Wi-Fi connection, with wording about
protecting the phone, and does not offer a skip. Joining a network is
the way through. Do not tell the log the network name. Do not accept a
system update if the wizard offers one. Skip accounts unless the wizard
demands the Google account from before the wipe. The next read is
`ro.boot.flash.locked` and `ro.boot.verifiedbootstate` once a shell
stays open. Fastboot must still answer after that.
